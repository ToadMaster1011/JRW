About This Property

Our cozy Two Pine Cottage is tucked away in the charming town of Watervliet, Michigan. It is just a 20-minute drive from South Haven and close to beaches, wineries, and local summer attractions.

The cottage is designed for relaxed group stays with flexible sleeping arrangements, a fully equipped kitchen, and a comfortable living area for movie nights or quiet mornings. You can enjoy morning coffee outside, grill in the backyard, and settle in with fast wifi after a day out.

Hosted by Jonathan Romero, this stay focuses on simple comfort, practical amenities, and easy self check-in so guests can arrive on their own schedule.
